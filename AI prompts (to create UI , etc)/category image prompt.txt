## Lighting & Shadow

Use soft premium studio lighting with a large diffused light source.

Include:

* Realistic ambient occlusion
* Soft contact shadows between objects
* Beautiful glossy reflections
* Subtle cinematic product-render lighting
* Elegant highlights that define the 3D shapes

Because the illustration will be displayed on a light background, add a **soft, clean and premium ground/contact shadow underneath the composition**.

The shadow should feel natural and refined, helping the objects visually sit within the composition without becoming heavy, dark or distracting. Use soft edges, subtle opacity and realistic diffusion for a polished website-ready appearance.
